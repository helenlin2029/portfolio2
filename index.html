Growing up

A sanctuary city of immigrants, driven by determination and work ethic.

I grew up in New York City, where my parents immigrated to from China. In the most diverse area in NYC, Queens, immigrants search for opportunities to improve the various aspects of their lives.

As a child, I spent much of my free time either watching PBS Kids, playing in my neighbor's backyard, or helping out at my uncle's restaurant. Commonly, many immigrants are foreign to the financial system in the United States and lack financial literacy. My first exposures to finance include discussing stocks with my sister, learning the concept of investing from PBS Kids, and conspiring on business models with my uncle.

When I was 10, my family moved to Michigan to start their own business. Now having spent all of my free time supporting my parents, I had exposure to the firsthand decisions that business owners made: the tradeoffs, optimizations, sacrifices, etc. This era of my life solidified my work ethic and influenced how I thought about making decisions.

At 12, we moved back to New York, where I eventually resumed my education following COVID.
